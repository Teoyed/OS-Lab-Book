\noindent This laboratory manual has been prepared for the Operating Systems Laboratory course at the University of Mazandaran. It is designed to provide students with practical instructions, laboratory exercises, and supplementary material for studying and experimenting with fundamental operating systems concepts.

\vspace{0.4cm}

\noindent The manual was prepared by Reza Ahmadizadeh in his capacity as Teaching Assistant for the course under the supervision of Dr. Afrabandpey. It is intended as a supplementary educational resource for students enrolled in the course.

\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent This manual is maintained as an open educational resource through its accompanying Git repository. The contents may be revised and updated between editions to correct errors, improve explanations, and incorporate additional laboratory material.
